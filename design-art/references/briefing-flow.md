# Design Art Briefing Flow

Use this before ImageGen when the product does not yet have an existing UI identity.

## Rule

Ask one high-impact question per turn. Do not dump a full intake questionnaire into chat.

## Goal

Turn a vague idea into an owner-approved page direction and generation brief.

## Required Coverage

- product;
- audience;
- surface type;
- primary job;
- page message;
- desired sections;
- must show;
- desired action;
- differentiation;
- visual direction;
- color posture;
- density;
- page length;
- brand status;
- existing `DESIGN.md` or design-context source;
- must include;
- must avoid.

These are coverage targets, not a form to paste.

## Owner UX

Use plain product language. Offer 3-5 answer options when a choice is useful, plus a "decide for me" escape hatch. After enough context is known, show a compact brief and a simple ASCII story map.

Do not run ImageGen until the owner approves the brief or explicitly delegates the decision.
